Issue #206 — second chair test backend log observation

Captured from the running backend terminal during the second chair test:

INFO:     ('192.168.5.171', 61665) - "WebSocket /ws/vision" [accepted]
INFO:routers.ai_service:[WS Vision] Connected: 192.168.5.171
INFO:     connection open

The session later reported:

ERROR:routers.ai_service:[WS Vision] Unexpected error (192.168.5.171): Unexpected ASGI message 'websocket.send', after sending 'websocket.close' or response already completed.
INFO:     connection closed

Interpretation:

- The mobile client reached and opened the live vision WebSocket successfully.
- The log does not print individual `detection_result` or `guidance_message` payloads, so it cannot independently show the spoken chair warning.
- The send-after-close message appears during connection teardown/reconnect and should be tracked as a separate robustness issue if it is user-visible.
- The phone video/audio remains the primary evidence for the chair detection and “Not safe to move forward / Hazard ahead: chair” feedback.
